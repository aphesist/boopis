# boopis trailer: treatment

**Concept.** boopis's mark is a pair of eyes, and so is every bot. The film opens on two eyes in the dark and follows
them as they become a team that reads what you mean, works with three tools inside a sandbox, climbs only frontier
models, works across the plans you already pay for, learns, and waits for your one-second hold before anything ships;
then the eyes go home into the wordmark.

**Length and grid.** 101.8 s: 42 bars at 100 BPM (a bar is 2.4 s) and a one-second tail. Every cut, word and sound
sits on that grid; the soundtrack reads its effects from the same cue list the picture writes.

**Look.** A pitch-black stage (the dock's own `#000`) that real boopis UI floats on: plates of the Card catalog preset,
the kit's own components drawn large, orbs drawn at their final size. Headlines in Libre Franklin 600, sentence case,
"boopis" always lowercase; mono for state words, tools, models and numbers. Brass only marks where you are, red only
what you owe. Motion on the app's own curves (`--spring`, `--ease`) and the dock's critically damped springs: no
overshoot anywhere except the logo's playful letters, its one big moment. Lab marks appear only where a lab takes
part; nothing boopis is built from is named.

## Sound

Procedural, 100 BPM in D minor: a drone and a sub heartbeat for the cold open; a four-chord pad (Dm9, Bbmaj7, Fmaj9,
Csus2); sine-sweep kicks, filtered-noise claps and hats; a sub bass that ducks under the kick; plucks for the orbs,
bells for approvals, ticks for typing and the record; a one-second riser locked to the hold; the music stops dead when
the eyes return, and only the logo's "boop" and blips play over a held chord before the final one.
