## 23 November 2026: owner's review of act 1

6. **The film moves to the light theme** ("the white theme ... goes with more people"): the product UI in its light
   theme, and the film's own ground, light, grain, shadows, orbs and type re-made for a light frame by the art director.
   The dark look in SC/storyboard/look.md is replaced; the beats, the script and the end card (b) stay.
7. **Every act is revised, the animations above all.** The owner found more than two visual bugs in act 1's 9.6 s.
   The bar is "100% premium and well made": no visible defect of any size at full resolution.
   Two of them, from the owner's frames:
   - **Double images.** Moving orbs, eyes and the cursor show a sharp ghost copy instead of a smooth blur. The renderer
     kept only the two shutter ends when the changed area was small. Motion blur must be smooth everywhere, or off.
     A strobe or double image is a hard failure.
   - **The mention chip in the request bubble** sits below the text's line, and the bubble's padding is uneven. Every
     piece of product UI on screen must match the real product's layout exactly: baselines, padding, alignment.
8. **Nothing goes to the owner without a frame-by-frame review at full resolution** by a critic who isn't the animator,
   and by the coordinator.
