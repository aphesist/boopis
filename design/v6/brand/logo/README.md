# boopis logo

**The symbol is the two oval eyes of a bot. The wordmark is "boopis" with the same two ovals as its o's.** One colour, drawn as paths, no
dependencies. The eyes are the bots' own (the vendored orb2 build, `vendor/orb-avatar.js`), so the logo has the bots' 16 states.
The design system's rules for it are in `design-system/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`, section 12.

Open `index.html` for the demo. Captures are in `captures/`.

## The logo

- **Symbol:** two solid oval eyes, the o's own box (527 x 550 font units, a superellipse of exponent 2.4) set 0.19 eye-widths apart, the orb's own eye spacing.
- **Wordmark:** `boopis` in Libre Franklin 600 (SIL OFL 1.1, as paths: no font is loaded), the "oo" being the two eyes. The eyes take the o's sidebearings, so the spacing is the font's.
- Oval everywhere: the wordmark at every size, the symbol, the icons, the static files. There is no other eye shape in the shipped logo.
- The eyes are the orb's: `state="happy"` or `morph('love')` turns them into the bots' arcs, hearts and bars. The wide ones make the letters give way; the letters otherwise never move for the eyes.

## Static or animated

**Static** (`still`) is for anything that labels: the title bar, headers, footers, lists, documents, the window icon, exports. Nothing moves.

**Animated** is for a logo that is the subject of the screen. The default element is animated, with **live eyes**: they glance on their own (the orb's
calm idle director, a step every 1.8 to 2.6 s), drift a little, and blink (the orb's own 0.155 s, now and then, sometimes twice). The eyes move on a hidden sphere,
so the far eye narrows and the near one widens; the letters do not move for it. `look` also makes the eyes follow the pointer (capped).

- One logo moves per screen, and never one that stays in a fixed header while someone reads.
- **Reduced motion** reduces and never freezes: the eyes take small steps (half the amplitude, no travel), state changes are a 150 ms crossfade, there is no blink, no loop and no playful letters.
- The logo rests while it is scrolled out of view (no frame loop).
- `blink="off"` and `gaze="off"` turn either half of the live eyes off; `still` turns everything off.

## Playful letters: big moments only

`letters="play"` makes every letter its own piece (springs, a squash before a hop, a 48 ms stagger, the i's dot with a bounce of its own; the letters lean with the eyes' gaze).
It is **opt-in**; the default is still letters. Big moments are exactly three:

1. a launch,
2. a site hero,
3. the app's first-run welcome.

**Never** in chrome (title bar, sidebar, headers, footers), in lists, in repeated UI or a loader that repeats, below a type size of 32 px (it switches itself off), with `still`, for the mark, or with reduced motion.
Bounds: a letter turns at most 7 degrees and hops at most 92 units, all inside the protection area; every frame a guard keeps at least 16 units (or 40% of the natural gap) between neighbours and eyes.

## Sizes

- **Wordmark: a type size of 20 px or more** (the font-size you set; the x-height is then 10.6 px). Below 20 px the element draws the mark by itself; the static files are never used below it.
- **Mark: 16 px wide or more.**
- **Playful letters: 32 px type size or more** (and only for a big moment).
- **App icon:** 16 to 1024 px. At 16, 24 and 32 the eyes are snapped to whole pixels and set larger (below), so two clear eyes survive.

## Protection area

0.28 of the type size on every side, about half the height of an eye. It holds the eyes' travel, the playful letters' hops and turns, and the widest states' make-way.
For the mark the same measure applies: a mark of width W has a type size of W / 1.156, so the area is 0.24 of its width. Nothing enters it: no text, no edge, no other mark.

## One colour

`currentColor` and nothing else: the ink of the surface (`--text-strong` on the frame, the dock's ink on the dock). The eyes and the letters are the same colour. No gradient, shadow, outline, second colour or
translucency. Tokens only: in the app never a literal colour. The static files carry the two inks as literals only because an SVG file cannot read custom properties: **dark ink `#0f0e0c`**
(Bone `--text-strong`, the darkest ink in `tokens.css`) and **light ink `#ffffff`** (Graphite and Typewriter `--text-strong`, the lightest). `build/icons.mjs` reads them from `tokens.css`; it does not type them.

## Don't

- Stretch, skew, rotate, outline, shadow, blur or put an effect on it.
- Recolour the eyes, or use two colours, or a gradient.
- Set the word in live text, or in another font, or with other letters (the logo is `boopis`).
- Use the wordmark below 20 px, or the playful letters below 32 px.
- Run `letters="play"` anywhere but a launch, a site hero or the first-run welcome; run two logos at once.
- Put it on a photo, a pattern or a gradient; crowd it inside the protection area; add a tagline lockup.
- Use the app icon inside the app as the mark: in the app the mark follows the theme.

## API

```html
<script src="boopis-logo.js"></script>
<boopis-logo style="font-size:48px"></boopis-logo>                         <!-- the wordmark, live eyes (animated) -->
<boopis-logo still style="font-size:20px"></boopis-logo>                    <!-- the wordmark, static: a header or a list -->
<boopis-logo mark still style="width:32px"></boopis-logo>                  <!-- the symbol -->
<boopis-logo look style="font-size:96px"></boopis-logo>                    <!-- eyes follow the pointer -->
<boopis-logo letters="play" animation="hero" loop style="font-size:160px"></boopis-logo>   <!-- a big moment -->
```

| Attribute | |
|---|---|
| `mark` | Only the two eyes. |
| `still` | One frame: no blink, no glance, no motion, time-based states held at t = 0. |
| `blink="off"`, `gaze="off"` | Turn the blink or the glances and drift off (both are on by default). |
| `look` | The eyes follow the pointer, capped (the orb's pointer rule). |
| `letters` | `still` (default) or `play` (opt-in, big moments only, from a 32 px type size). |
| `state` | Initial state, one of `BoopisLogo.states`. |
| `animation="hero"`, `loop` | Play the hero when connected; repeat with a rest. |
| `word` | Default `boopis`. Another lowercase word with "oo" exists for tests only: the logo is boopis. |
| `weight` | 500 or 600 (default 600). |
| `label` | Accessible name (default `boopis`). |

Size it with `font-size` (the type size, in px); `width` also works (the mark is usually sized that way). The colour is `color`.

```js
logo.morph('happy')            // from the shape on screen to a state; never a cut, safe in bursts
logo.morph(null)               // back to idle
logo.follow('listening', true) // hover/focus; false returns to idle only if still the target
await logo.play('hero')        // resolves when the eyes are idle again
await logo.play(['listening', { state: 'thinking', hold: 1.4, blink: 0.8 }, ['arc', 'arc']], { stepDuration: 0.9 })
logo.look(0.5, 0.2); logo.look()   // glance right and a little up; let go
logo.fidget()                  // letters="play": one idle fidget now
logo.stop(); logo.blink()
logo.addEventListener('boopisstep', (e) => e.detail)   // { state, index }
BoopisLogo.states; BoopisLogo.sequences; BoopisLogo.timing; BoopisLogo.minEm; BoopisLogo.minPlayEm; BoopisLogo.protection
BoopisLogo.svg({ color: '#000' })              // a static wordmark SVG string; { mark: true }, { state: 'happy' } also work
```

States are the orb's 16 reactions (idle, listening, thinking, compute, working, speaking, happy, done, needs, sleeping, error, wink, skeptic, love, sad, dizzy), any orb eye shape or a `[left, right]` pair.
Timings: morph 0.38 s, blink 0.155 s, crossfade 0.15 s, hero rest 1.3 s. The hero loop is listening 1.1 s, thinking 1.7 s, working 1.7 s, happy 1.5 s, idle 0.4 s, rest: about 8 s.
In React, `boopis-logo.d.ts` types the element (`ref`, the attributes) and `window.BoopisLogo`.

## App icon

Two versions that coexist, on a continuous-corner tile (the design system's `HX.ui.squircle`, lifted unchanged from `components/components.js` by `build/icons.mjs`; **smoothing 0.6, corner radius 22.37% of the tile**,
the Apple icon grid's proportion: a proposal, not a design-system token). Plain ink, no cut-outs, no gradient, no shadow.

| | Tile | Eyes | For |
|---|---|---|---|
| `icons/light/` | `#ffffff`, the lightest ink (Graphite and Typewriter `--text-strong`) | `#0f0e0c`, the darkest ink (Bone `--text-strong`) | the light theme; the default icon |
| `icons/dark/` | `#0f0e0c`, the darkest ink | `#ffffff`, the lightest ink | the dark theme |

Pure `#000000` is `--dock-bg`, a surface and not an ink, so it is not used.

- **Standard sizes (128 and up):** the pair is 64% of the tile wide, centred.
- **Whole pixels (16 to 64):** the eyes are fitted to the pixel grid, width, height, gap and margins in whole or half pixels, and set larger.
  16 px: eyes 5 x 6, gap 2 (the pair is 75% of the tile). 24: 8 x 9, gap 2. 32: 10 x 11, gap 4. 48: 15 x 16, gap 4. 64: 20 x 21, gap 4.
- **Legibility:** checked magnified at 16 and 32 on both versions: two separate eyes, a clear gap, no merged edge. At 16 the oval is a 5 x 6 block with softened corners; it reads as two eyes, not as a "oo" shape.
- A white tile has no edge on a white surface (and a black one on a black surface). The icon adds nothing to it: the system's dock, shadow or plate does.

### Platform notes

| | What the system supports | What the app ships |
|---|---|---|
| **macOS** | Light, dark and tinted app icons, made in Icon Composer (one layered `.icon` document; macOS 26). Older macOS shows one `.icns`. | `icons/tauri-light/icon.icns`: the light icon on Apple's grid (the tile at 824 of 1024 px, centred, no shadow). | **Windows** | Light and dark taskbar icons: `altform` assets (`...targetsize-N_altform-lightunplated.png` for the light taskbar, `..._altform-unplated.png` for the dark one), in packaged (MSIX) apps. ### Tauri files

`icon.ico` and `icon.icns` are written by `build/icons-png.mjs` itself (the ICO and ICNS containers are simple; the entries are the PNGs rendered by Chromium). **`icon.icns` exists:** `icp4`, `icp5`, `icp6`, `ic07` to `ic10` and the @2x `ic11` to `ic14`,
read back with Pillow (all 11 entries). ImageMagick here has no ICNS delegate, `png2icns` and `iconutil` are not installed. On a Mac, to rebuild it with Apple's tool from `icons/light/` (not on the Apple grid):

```sh
mkdir boopis.iconset
for s in 16 32 128 256 512; do
  cp icons/light/boopis-icon-light-$s.png     boopis.iconset/icon_${s}x${s}.png
  cp icons/light/boopis-icon-light-$((s*2)).png boopis.iconset/icon_${s}x${s}@2x.png
done
iconutil -c icns boopis.iconset -o icon.icns
```

## Proposals (not in the design system)

1. The icon corner (22.37%), the optical sizes of the icon (64% standard, 75% at 16 to 32), the macOS grid for the `.icns`, and the light icon as the default on every platform.
2. Protection area 0.28 of the type size; minimum sizes 20 px (wordmark), 16 px (mark), 32 px (playful letters); one moving logo per screen.
3. The capped gaze (yaw .30, pitch .20), the travel budgets, and keeping the sphere projection for shape only (so the letters never move for it).
4. The stricter reduced-motion behaviour (no blink, half-size steps only).
5. Live eyes as the default of the element, with `still` for labels.
