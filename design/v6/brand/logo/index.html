Wordmark

The type size is the font-size you set. Twenty pixels is the smallest: below it the element draws the mark by itself. Shown here still, in the light ink and the dark ink.

Mark

The two eyes alone, for places too small for the wordmark: avatars, favicons, the sidebar rail, the title bar. 16 pixels wide is the smallest.

App icon

Two versions that coexist, on the continuous-corner tile (smoothing 0.6, corner 22.37% of the tile): black eyes on white for the light theme, white eyes on black for the dark theme. Plain ink, no cut-outs. Below 64 pixels the eyes are snapped to whole pixels and set larger, so two clear eyes survive at 16 and 32.

Every state, still

The bots' own 16 reactions, one frame each (time-based states are held at their first frame). The wide ones make the letters give way; nothing else moves. In the app they play only where a bot is the subject.

Static or animated

Static is the default for anything that labels: the title bar, headers, footers, lists, documents, the window icon, exports. Pass still. Animated is for a logo that is the subject of the screen: the eyes are then live, glancing and blinking on their own, and following the pointer with look.

  • One logo moves per screen, and never one that stays in a fixed header while someone reads.
  • Under reduced motion the eyes take small steps and no blink: reduced, never frozen. The playful letters do not run at all.
  • The logo rests while it is scrolled out of view.

Playful letters: big moments only

letters="play" is opt-in and the default stays still. Big moments are three: a launch, a site hero, the app's first-run welcome. Never in chrome, in lists, in a loading state that repeats, in a header, or below a type size of 32 pixels (where it is switched off by itself).

Protection area and size

Keep 0.28 of the type size clear on every side, about half the height of an eye. It holds the eyes' travel and the playful letters' hops and turns. For the mark the same measure applies: a mark of width W has a type size of W / 1.156, so 0.24 of its width.

One colour

currentColor and nothing else: the ink of the surface it sits on (--text-strong on the frame, the dock's ink on the dock). The eyes and the letters are the same colour. No gradient, shadow, outline or second colour. Tokens only, never a literal.
