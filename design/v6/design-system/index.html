One cabinet, four finishes

boopis is a desktop harness for AI coding agents. This page is its design system drawn by itself: every swatch, number and component below comes from the same tokens and the same library the app loads, so the theme controls above re-theme the whole page.

Model, time and cost live in a fixed margin. Brass marks where you are; red marks what you owe. Everything else stays quiet until it is needed.

Principles

Each one is a rule with a reason. When two collide, the higher one wins.

  1. The conversation is the workThe column, the request and the answer take the ground; chrome, panels and the dock recede until called. Nothing in the frame competes with the measure.
  2. Interrupt only for decisionsRed, motion and sound are spent on the user's attention: a blank waiting, an approval, a failed gate. Everything else is quiet, so those three are never missed.
  3. Cost and model are always knowable, never loudThey sit in the call-number margin and in running totals against their limit, in mono, in the same place every time.
  4. Every path has a second pathThe dock, the sidebars and the palette reach the same things, and the keyboard reaches all of them. No component is the only way to do something.
  5. The agent is a tool, bots are teammatesAn answer shows only the model that wrote it. Bots have a name, a role and a face, always in that order and always in the same places.
  6. Standard controls, one signatureButtons, rows, tabs and menus work the way a developer already expects. The world lends palette, type, density and the call-number margin; it never supplies the controls.

Voice

Plain words a developer would say out loud, in sentence case. Labels name the thing or the action; state words are short and lowercase.

  • Sentence case for interface text: New session, Show the dock, Hold to approve. The brand is always lowercase: boopis. Content keeps its own case.
  • Buttons are a verb and its object: Open PR, Fix and retry, Raise cap to $1.00, Schedule it. Never OK, Submit or Learn more.
  • State words are one or two words in mono: working, thinking, replying, needs you, done, asleep, failed. Idle shows nothing.
  • Stamps have three wordings only: waiting for you, needs you, failed.
  • Receipts say what happened and when: Approved at 14:09. Expired, nothing ran. Errors name the problem and the way out.
  • No all caps, middle-dot meta strings, em dashes, exclamation marks or arrows after links. The arrow in a model escalation (qwen3-coder → sonnet 5.5) is content.

Colour

Surfaces separate by tone, not by shadow. Two colours carry meaning and nothing else: brass for where you are, red for what you owe. The values below are read from the page as it renders, so they follow the preset and any override.

Surfaces, deepest to highest

The frame holds the work; the ground is what the work sits on; raised card stock is what you wrote; inset drawer is what is set into the work.

Roles

Derived lines and fills

Translucent tokens are mixed from the text colour, so a theme only chooses solid colours and every line follows it.

Contrast

The pairs every theme must hold, measured live against the current preset and overrides: 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for icons, accents, outlines and focus. The customizer runs the same check as the user edits a colour.

Type

One family per role at fixed steps: a gothic sans for interface and prose, a mono for code, paths, numbers, times, costs, model names and call numbers. Six sizes, three weights, nothing in between.

Space and shape

A 4px base with seven steps; rows and controls change with density; four corner radii change with the corner setting. Corners are Apple's continuous corners, drawn the same in every engine, and a corner inside another fits it. The capsule is for chips, dots, the switch and what sits inside a pill.

Corners

Owner decisions. Apple's continuous corners, in every engine: the corner is drawn as a path (Figma's corner smoothing at 0.6, as iOS), because WebKit, which Tauri uses on Linux and macOS, has no corner-shape. Radii are generous: sheets and the dock 30, cards 20, buttons, rows and fields 12, chips a capsule. A rounded surface inside another fits it: the inner radius is the outer less the gap, never under a floor of 8; where the arithmetic would go under it, the padding gives way, not the radius.

Elevation and lines

Flat. Hairlines divide inside a surface, lines outline controls, field lines bound text fields at 3:1. Shadows only on things that float over other things.

Motion

Motion explains a change of state and nothing else. No curve passes its mark. The one loop is a running dot breathing. Cards are the one place height animates, so the conversation never jumps. Under reduced motion everything snaps and the loop stops; the orbs reduce and never freeze (owner decision), and the one you are looking at keeps a small step every 1.8s.

Icons and mark

Drawn on a 20 or 16 unit grid and rendered at one stroke, 1.5px, with round caps, in the current colour. 18px in icon buttons, 16px in rows and buttons, 14px in small spots.

The φ mark

The closed φ, filled in the strongest neutral of the surface it sits on. Never red, never brass, never outlined, never under 16px wide.

Bots and orbs

Every bot has the same furniture in the same places: orb, name, role, schedule, state word. The orb's face reacts to the bot's state through one map; the words beside it come from the same map, so the list, the dock, the header and the cards never disagree.

States

One map from a bot's state to its face and its word. Attention outranks work.

Sizes

16 in minis and schedule rows (still), 20 in lists and call numbers, 24 in cards, 26 in the roster, 40 in the dock and the bot header, 56 in the dock card. Live orbs drift calmly; only the one you are looking at moves livelier.

Components

One canonical version of each, from the shared library. Every state below is live: hover, focus with the keyboard, press.

Buttons

Primary is bone on ink, once per group. Secondary is an outline. Ghost is for the quiet way out. Icon buttons are 28px with an 18px glyph and always carry a tooltip.

Hold to approve

The one control that commits by holding: card stock at rest, a dot field gathers under the press and fuses into bone. One second, everywhere (owner decision). Keyboard: hold Space, or press Enter twice.

Switches and choices

A switch for on and off; a segmented control for two to four exclusive values or views, with a thumb that slides; chips for modes inside a toolbar; option chips for presets.

Marks

Code chips, keys, the stamp, and the status dots: running breathes, attention is red, here is brass, idle is nothing at all.

Lists

One row for every sidebar list, menu-like or not. Glyphs centre on x = 28, labels start at x = 44, meta is mono on the right. The current row is filled; actions replace the meta on hover.

Menus, tooltips, toasts

Floating surfaces sit on the frame colour with a line and the pop shadow. Menus drop 6px over 200ms; tooltips wait 480ms the first time and then follow at once.

Fields and settings

Drawer colour on the frame, card stock on the ground, a field line at 3:1, the focus ring on the field. Settings rows put the label left and the control right.

Ladder and measures

The governance ladder is a line with stations, the current one brass. Capacities are a running total against the limit in mono. Context is dots, never a bar.

Conversation

Each entry puts its call number in the 112px margin and its content in the 608px measure. What you wrote is a request card; the answer is prose; a blank waits in its sentence with its stamp.

Composer

Card stock with a hairline; its actions sit in its corner and fit it. Every composer can talk: the mic, or alt+V (a tap toggles, a hold talks while held); with a bot as the target its orb listens. The compact one is the dock's. Dashed words are the agent's reading of the request; solid brass is your choice. Enter sends, Shift and Enter break the line.

Right panel

Closed, it is a 28px strip with one quiet measure. Open, it is the frame colour with sections that disclose.

Cards

Set into the conversation in the drawer colour, 520px at most. One card element, eight kinds, three sizes, every state; the decided ones recede. Red is only the stamp of what you owe.

Shell

Title bar with the command field, the sidebar open, as a rail and closed, the ground meeting the frame with a concave corner.

Dock

Pitch black in every theme, flush to the bottom edge with concave flares, always centred with the composer. Everything it shows is reachable elsewhere. Its contents use the same components on the dock ground. The orbs answer the owner's gestures: a click or Enter opens the bot's session in the dock (again closes it), a double-click or mod+Enter opens its full chat in the Bots tab.
