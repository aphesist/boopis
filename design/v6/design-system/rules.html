Every decision has a rule

Version 1.1 of the boopis design system gives each visual decision a written rule with an ID, so a fix, a review or an automated check can cite it. This page draws the new and tightened rules live, from the same tokens and library the app loads; the controls above re-theme it. The rules themselves are in DESIGN-SYSTEM.md, one line each with their checks in RULES.md.

Every rule here is approved; the ones marked were settled in the review of 19 November 2026.

Scrollbars

One thin bar in every engine, drawn with ::-webkit-scrollbar, because WebKitGTK ignores scrollbar-width and Chromium turns the pseudo-elements off while it is set. A 4px capsule in a 10px gutter that is always there, so nothing moves when the bar appears; 6px and 3:1 under the pointer; its track stops short of the dialog's curve.

Corners and tabs

A piece in a corner sits inside it and fits it; the pieces on the same band as the corner piece share its radius. In a dialog the close button sits 12 from both edges at 30 − 12 = 18, so the head's tabs take 18 too: at 28px tall they are capsules, concentric with the dialog. A segmented control on that band takes the band's radius for its track.

The composer's corner pieces

Attach, chips, mic and send sit 6 from the box's edges at 20 − 6 = 14; nothing touches the edge or its curve.

Surfaces on surfaces

Depth is tone. In flow, each surface holds only the next tone of the ladder; a shadow appears only when something floats, in five levels. A card never holds a card: it holds a well. Cards waiting in a queue stack as peeks of their foot, inset 12 and 24, at .9 and .55, with no shadow.

Type in use

Six sizes, three weights by role, line heights from tokens. A one-line label ends with an ellipsis and never wraps.

Icons

Six sizes, each a token, one 1.5px stroke at every size. Icon to label is 8 in buttons, rows and menus, 6 in chips and controls of 26px and under. A glyph is centred in its own square; in a two-line menu item it centres on the first line.

Keys and search fields

A key chip at the end of a rounded container takes that container's inner radius, never more than a capsule: 8 in a tooltip and in the 26px command field. In running text it keeps 6. A search field's glyph is centred in a square as tall as the field, as the palette already does.

Focus and states

Every control draws the same states with the same tokens. Hover and press are shown here as they look; focus is the 2px brass ring, inset inside rows and tracks. Disabled controls drawn in fills share one opacity.
